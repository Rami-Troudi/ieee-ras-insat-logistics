# IEEE RAS INSAT Logistics — Context

> **Property of IEEE INSAT Student Branch**  
> **Do not redistribute without permission.**

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the logistics framework governing the borrowing, handling, returning, and management of items belonging to **IEEE RAS INSAT**.

The objectives are to:

- Ensure responsible use of RAS resources.
- Prevent damage, misuse, or loss.
- Maintain fairness and accountability.
- Define access rights according to user clearance.
- Establish disciplinary and compensation procedures.
- Define the responsibilities of both users and logistics management.

---

# 2. Item Classification

RAS logistics items are divided into seven classes.

## Class A — Consumables

Items that are consumed during normal use.

Examples:

- Hot glue
- Strong glue
- Adhesive

## Class B — Expendable Resources

Minor, lightweight, and easily replaceable items.

Examples:

- Screws
- LEDs
- Resistors
- Rods
- Cables

## Class C — Light Resources

Small, countable, usually non-electronic parts.

Examples:

- Bearings
- Motor supports
- Wheels
- Batteries

## Class D — Light Equipment

Light tools, primarily non-electric, with minor injury risks.

Examples:

- Screwdrivers
- Hammers
- Wrenches
- Multimeters

## Class E — Electronic Resources

Electronic and sensitive items, often PCB-based.

Examples:

- Arduino boards
- STM boards
- Sensors
- Motors

## Class F — Heavy Equipment

Tools that may cause severe injury and require experience to operate.

Examples:

- Drill
- Grinder
- Soldering iron

## Class G — High-Value Electronics

Electronic equipment considered:

- Irreplaceable
- Rare
- Unavailable or difficult to obtain on the common market

# 3. Clearance Levels

Access to logistics resources depends on the individual's clearance level.

| Level | Category | Allowed Access |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | External Individuals | Classes A and B |
| **II** | Aerobotix Members | Classes A, B, and C |
| **III** | IEEE Members | Classes A, B, C, D, and E. Class F only under Level V+ supervision |
| **IV** | Trusted Individuals | All classes |
| **V** | RAS Board and Eurobot Members | Logistics management privileges; may grant items according to policy and supervise Class F usage |
| **VI** | RAS Chairman and Logistics Manager | Highest logistics authority; may grant resources at their discretion and are the only authority allowed to grant Class G items |

## Level I — External Individuals

Definition:

Anyone who does not belong to IEEE or Aerobotix.

Allowed:

- Class A
- Class B

## Level II — Aerobotix Members

Definition:

Anyone with an active Aerobotix membership.

Allowed:

- Class A
- Class B
- Class C

## Level III — IEEE Members

Definition:

Anyone with an active IEEE membership.

Allowed:

- Class A
- Class B
- Class C
- Class D
- Class E

Class F items may only be used under the supervision of someone with **Level V or higher clearance**.

## Level IV — Trusted Individuals

Special clearance granted by a **Level VI official** to specific trusted individuals.

Allowed:

- All item classes

## Level V — RAS Board and Eurobot Members

Reserved for individuals involved in managing chapter logistics.

Permissions include:

- Granting items while respecting these policies.
- Supervising the use of Class F items.

## Level VI — RAS Chairman and Logistics Manager

Highest logistics authority.

Their decisions regarding logistics are final.

Permissions include:

- Granting resources at their discretion.
- Granting Class G items.

Only Level VI authorities may grant **Class G** resources.

# 4. Logistics Usage Policy

All IEEE members may request RAS materials, subject to their clearance level and the procedures defined below.

## 4.1 Borrowing Procedure

### Class A

Procedure:

1. Complete the borrowing form.
2. Request the item from any RAS Board or Eurobot member.

Usage:

- Unrestricted.

### Class B

Procedure:

1. Request the item directly from any RAS Board or Eurobot member.

Usage:

- Unrestricted.

Return requirement:

- Must be returned after final use.

### Class C

Procedure:

1. Complete the borrowing form.
2. Request the item from any RAS Board or Eurobot member.

Usage:

- Unrestricted as long as the item is returned in its original state.

### Class D

Procedure:

1. Request the item directly from the Board.

Return requirement:

- Must be returned to its original location after use.

Failure to return the item to its original location results in a **strike**.

### Class E

Procedure:

1. Complete the borrowing form.
2. Wait for notification from the Board before receiving or using the item.

### Class F

For IEEE members:

1. Submit the borrowing form.
2. Receive Board notification.
3. Use the item only under supervision.

For users with **Level IV clearance**:

- Class F items may be requested without the Level V supervision requirement imposed on ordinary IEEE members.

---

# 5. Returning Items

The same logistics form used for borrowing is also used for returning items.

General return procedure applies to all classes, with the following specific rules:

## Class B

- Must be returned after final use.

## Class D

- Must be returned to its original location immediately after use.

---

# 6. Disciplinary Actions

## 6.1 Irreversible Damage or Destruction

Any irreversible damage to **Class D or higher** items results in a strike.

For non-IEEE members:

- A strike-related consequence may apply where relevant.
- Compensation is also required.

## 6.2 Failure to Surrender Borrowed Items

If an item is not returned within **two weeks after the due date**, the responsible individual receives a strike.

## 6.3 Breach of Borrowing Policies

Any violation of the logistics policies results in an immediate strike.

This includes:

- Ignoring procedures.
- Circumventing the policy.
- Exploiting loopholes.

## 6.4 Reckless Endangerment

Any behavior that:

- Endangers individuals, or
- Compromises IEEE INSAT's interests while using RAS resources

results in a **direct ban**.

---

# 7. Strike System

## Strike 1 — Warning

Consequence:

- Warning only.
- No direct restriction.

## Strike 2 — Second Warning

Consequences:

- Borrowing privileges are restricted.
- Compensation may be imposed.

## Strike 3 — Last Warning

Consequence:

- The user may no longer use Class E items unsupervised.

## Strike 4 — Disciplinary Ban

Consequence:

- The user is banned from using any RAS item.

## Strike 5 — Permanent Ban

Consequence:

- Permanent ban from using RAS logistics.

## Strike Expiration

Strikes expire every semester.

Exception:

- Blacklists do **not** expire automatically.

---

# 8. Compensation Policy

## 8.1 Determining Compensation

Compensation is based on the **full replacement cost** of the damaged or lost item.

Any replacement must provide equivalent:

- Quality
- Functionality

The **Logistics Manager** determines whether a replacement is acceptable.

## 8.2 Payment Timeline

Compensation must be settled within the period determined by the Logistics Manager.

Typical timeframe:

- **2 to 4 weeks**

Failure to compensate within the required timeframe may result in:

- Additional strikes
- Additional restrictions
- A ban

## 8.3 Appeals

If an individual believes a compensation decision is unfair, they may appeal to the **RAS Chairman**.

The Chairman's decision after appeal is final.

# 10. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for the following.

## Proper Handling

Users must:

- Handle all items carefully.
- Follow safety guidelines.
- Follow usage instructions.

## Timely Returns

Users must:

- Return items by the agreed deadline.
- Complete the required logistics forms.

## Honest Reporting

Users must immediately report any:

- Damage
- Loss
- Malfunction

Reports must be made to:

- The RAS Board, or
- The Logistics Manager

---

# 11. Management Responsibilities

The RAS Board and Logistics Manager are responsible for the following.

## Item Maintenance

They must:

- Keep items in good working condition.
- Perform periodic checks.

## Record Keeping

They must accurately maintain:

- Inventory records
- Borrowing records
- Strike history

## Fair Enforcement

They must apply:

- Strikes
- Compensation requests
- Disciplinary actions

in a consistent and transparent manner.

## Continuous Improvement

The Logistics Manager and Chairman may update logistics policies when required.

Updates should ensure that policies remain:

- Clear
- Fair
- Adapted to evolving logistics needs

---

# 12. Decision Authority

For logistics-related decisions:

- **RAS Board / Eurobot members** may grant items according to the rules associated with their clearance.
- **Level V personnel** may supervise Class F usage.
- **Level VI personnel** are the highest logistics authority.
- Only **Level VI** personnel may grant Class G items.
- Compensation acceptability is determined by the **Logistics Manager**.
- Compensation appeals are decided by the **RAS Chairman**, whose appeal decision is final.

---

# 13. Operational Rules Summary

When implementing or enforcing this policy, the following rules should be treated as hard constraints:

1. Never grant an item above the requester's permitted clearance level.
2. Class G items require Level VI approval.
3. Ordinary IEEE members require Level V+ supervision when using Class F equipment.
4. Class D items must be returned to their original location after use.
5. Class B items must be returned after final use.
6. Borrowing and returning procedures must follow the required form/notification process for each class.
7. Class D+ irreversible damage triggers a strike.
8. Items overdue by more than two weeks trigger a strike.
9. Any borrowing-policy violation, including loophole exploitation, triggers a strike.
10. Reckless endangerment results in a direct ban.
11. Compensation is based on full replacement cost.
12. Strike history must be tracked.
13. Ordinary strikes expire every semester; blacklist status does not.
14. Inventory and borrowing records must remain accurate and traceable.
